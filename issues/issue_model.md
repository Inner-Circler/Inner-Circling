## What is here

An **issue** is a **locus** — a place where physical, emotional or mental energy
concentrates, carrying risk or opportunity — felt as something **owed**. Owing
runs three ways:

- **owed to the operator** — recognition, ease, love, a want allowed to count
- **owed by the operator** — the ledger's own grammar, a debt to discharge
- **owed to the self, by the self** — due diligence, care, respect, **and
  capacity left unopened**. Repeatedly choosing an unhealthy action violates
  this, and the repetition is the evidence. So does letting a real possibility
  pass unexamined. A standing *watch* exists because something is being
  violated, not because a rule needs restating.

**This is not a ledger.** A locus is recorded so a choice can be made knowingly,
not so a failure can be scored. Record **"I want"**, never **"I do not
deserve"** — a want is a fact about a person, desert is a verdict about their
worth, and the second is the deserving-ledger this graph exists partly to
watch. A locus seen and deliberately not pursued is **declined**: a completed
act of agency, closed, carrying nothing forward. Declining is not deferring
and it is not failing.

**Not every charged thing is a locus.** A role, title or circumstance with no
felt charge and no instance is a *category*, not an issue.

Actions taken toward an issue are properties of that issue, not issues
themselves. Projects, rhythms and rules are not issues. Part-to-part relations
are not recorded anywhere; they show in the circle itself. Honesty is an
expectation held in process_core.md, not an issue for resolution.

A **best practice** is the inverse: a behaviour to encourage. It is never
resolved and never settles.
